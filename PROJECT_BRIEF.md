# Signal Fracture — Project Brief

## Positioning

**Chaos engineering for human communication.**

## One-sentence pitch

Signal Fracture runs fictional coordination drills through the communication channels a team already uses, deliberately fractures their information, and reconstructs exactly where coordination failed.

## Core reveal

Three people make individually reasonable decisions:

- Field seals Bay 3 on Telegram.
- Control routes a crew through Bay 3 on Discord using stale information.
- Director delays escalation by Email.

The dashboard draws the contradiction in real time.

## Why Caspian is central

- Different roles naturally operate in different channels.
- One shared handler observes every response.
- Each conversation receives a private, role-specific truth.
- Proactive sends alter the scenario across channels.
- Caspian conversation IDs bind durable role identity.
- The project demonstrates agent-to-human communication, not just notifications.

## MVP

- One fixed fictional scenario
- Three roles
- Telegram, Discord, Email
- Text commands
- Deterministic scenario and contradiction engines
- Convex persistence/outbox
- Live causal dashboard
- After-action timeline

## Non-goals

- Real emergency dispatch
- Real industrial control
- Medical/legal/security advice
- General scenario authoring platform
- Voice/SMS
- Large simulation engine
- Autonomous operational decisions
